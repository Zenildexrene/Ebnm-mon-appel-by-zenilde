# Application Note
This application was created to make the book "Mon Appel" (My Call) easily accessible to all believers and specifically to KCC youth.

This precious testimony retraces the spiritual events marking the calling of Prophet DIZOLELE-MPUNGU WAFIDUSWA Isaac, chosen by the Lord to fulfill a divine mission for the salvation of souls.

# Preface
Human nature is complex, but among ordinary people, prophets of the Lord stand out as chosen and consecrated before birth, like the Prophet Jeremiah (Jer. 1:5).

The calling of DIZOLELE-MPUNGU WAFIDUSWA Isaac by the Lord reflects the path of an anointed man predestined to bring lost souls back to God.

# Introduction
The sacred mission of bringing souls back to God belongs to Prophets called by the Most High Himself, such as Moses, Samuel, Isaiah, and Ezekiel.

After more than half a century of prophetic ministry, by divine command, I present this account of the spiritual events that marked my calling by the Lord.

# I. Context of My Call
In February 1955, I traveled to FORAKAM in Seke-Banza in search of employment. I was welcomed at Camp Teli by my friend MVIKA Michel.

During this period of waiting at Camp Teli, extraordinary spiritual events and visions began to manifest in my life.

[IMG:img_mvika_michel|MVIKA Michel, the man who welcomed and hosted me at Camp Teli]

# II. Apparitions
On the night of March 7 to 8, 1955, at around 1:00 AM, a voice called me: "Isaac!" I answered "Yes" and found myself standing beside a man who said, "Follow me."

2.1. FIRST APPARITION
He showed me a deep well of crystal-clear water with large fish. He asked me to dip my finger into the water, symbolizing spiritual abundance and my mission to feed and guide God's people.

2.2. SECOND AND THIRD APPARITIONS
He revealed the responsibilities of my calling and directed me toward Boma to spread the Word of God.
